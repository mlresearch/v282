title: Neural Fields Meet Attention
abstract: We establish a mathematical connection between neural field optimization
  and Transformer attention mechanics. First, we prove that Transformer-based operators
  learning a neural field are equivariant to affine transformations (translations
  and positive scalings) when using relative positional encodings and coordinate normalization,
  extending geometric deep learning to meta-learning of continuous functions. Second,
  we demonstrate that linear attention is an exact computation of the negative gradient
  of squared-error loss for sinusoidal neural fields, with softmax attention shown
  empirically and theoretically to converge to such an identity at rate $O(\tau^{-2})$
  as temperature scales. The novel results reveal that attention mechanisms have an
  implicit geometric encoding that is well-suited to learn continuous functions.
