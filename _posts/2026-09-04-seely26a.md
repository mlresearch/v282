title: Sheaf Cohomology of Linear Predictive Coding Networks
abstract: 'Predictive coding (PC) replaces global backpropagation with local optimization
  over weights and activations. We show that linear PC networks admit a natural formulation
  as cellular sheaves: the sheaf coboundary maps activations to edge-wise prediction
  errors, and PC inference is diffusion under the sheaf Laplacian. Sheaf cohomology
  then characterizes irreducible error patterns that inference cannot remove. We analyze
  recurrent topologies where feedback loops create internal contradictions, introducing
  prediction errors unrelated to supervision. Using a Hodge decomposition, we determine
  when these contradictions cause learning to stall. The sheaf formalism provides
  both diagnostic tools for identifying problematic network configurations and design
  principles for effective weight initialization for recurrent PC networks.'
